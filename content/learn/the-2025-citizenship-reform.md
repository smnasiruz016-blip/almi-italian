This page used to say 31 May 2026. That date is no longer the deadline for minors' declarations. It was moved to **31 May 2029** by a law of 27 February 2026, and the Ministero dell'Interno circularised the change on 12 March 2026. If you read an earlier version of this page, or any guide written between mid-2025 and early 2026, check the date you took away from it against the table below.

## The dates, and what each one is a deadline for

| Date | What it is a deadline for | Instrument | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 27 March 2025, 23:59 Rome time | The cut-off by which certain conditions had to be met for *iure sanguinis* recognition to survive the new restriction, including an application lodged with a consulate or a mayor | D.L. 28 marzo 2025, n. 36, as converted | Past |
| 24 May 2025 | Entry into force of the conversion law; also the reference date for identifying which children count as minors | Legge 23 maggio 2025, n. 74 | Past |
| 1 July 2025 – 31 December 2027 | The window in which declarations of *riacquisto* (reacquisition) may be lodged | Reported by MAECI consular notices | Open |
| 31 May 2029 | The deadline for the declaration under art. 4, comma 1-bis, lett. b) L. 91/1992, for children who were minors as at 24 May 2025 | Legge 27 febbraio 2026, n. 26 | Open — replaces 31 May 2026 |

## What the 2025 law did

The instrument is **Legge 23 maggio 2025, n. 74**, whose Gazzetta Ufficiale title is *"Conversione in legge, con modificazioni, del decreto-legge 28 marzo 2025, n. 36, recante disposizioni urgenti in materia di cittadinanza"* — conversion into law, with modifications, of decree-law 28 March 2025 no. 36, on urgent provisions concerning citizenship. It entered into force on **24 May 2025**.

Its central move, per the *testo coordinato* published on Normattiva, is a new **art. 3-bis** of Law 91/1992 restricting recognition *iure sanguinis* (by descent). We describe it as the coordinated text describes it, rather than quoting, because the passage came back to us reworded: a person born abroad who also holds another citizenship is treated as never having acquired Italian citizenship, unless specified conditions were satisfied by 27 March 2025. Those conditions include an application lodged with a consulate or a mayor by 23:59 Rome time on that date; a judicial recognition of status; an ancestor of the first or second degree who held Italian citizenship exclusively; or a parent or adoptive parent resident in Italy for at least two continuous years before the birth or adoption.

That is a summary, not legal advice. If any of those conditions may apply to you, read the coordinated text yourself and take advice.

## The 2026 correction

The extension came in **Legge 27 febbraio 2026, n. 26**, titled *"Conversione in legge, con modificazioni, del decreto-legge 31 dicembre 2025, n. 200, recante disposizioni urgenti in materia di termini normativi"*. It was published in Gazzetta Ufficiale Serie Generale n. 49 on 28 February 2026 and entered into force on **1 March 2026**.

A consular notice states the new deadline and the group it applies to:

> *"I genitori italiani di figli minori al 24 maggio 2025"*
>
> "Italian parents of children who were minors as at 24 May 2025."

For that group the declaration deadline is now 31 May 2029. A second consulate records the mechanism: article 1, comma 19-ter of the conversion law amends article 1, comma 1-ter of D.L. 36/2025, moving the date from 31 May 2026 to 31 May 2029. The Ministero dell'Interno issued **Circolare n. 19951 del 12 marzo 2026**, headed *"Cittadinanza per beneficio di legge in favore di figli minori di cittadini italiani per nascita. Proroga termine dichiarazioni"*, citing the same law.

### Two date discrepancies worth knowing about

Neither changes the substance, but both will confuse you if you meet them cold.

- **The law's own date.** The Gazzetta Ufficiale and the Ministry's circolare page both say *"legge 27 febbraio 2026, n. 26"*. Several MAECI consular notices say *"Legge 28 febbraio 2026, n. 26"* — which appears to be the publication date. Cite 27 February 2026, n. 26, published 28 February 2026, in force 1 March 2026.
- **The decree it converted.** The Gazzetta Ufficiale and the Ministry circolare give *"decreto-legge 31 dicembre 2025, n. 200"*. A Normattiva index title returned *"decreto-legge 1 dicembre 2025, n. 200"*. We prefer the Gazzetta Ufficiale form, and note the discrepancy exists on Normattiva's own listing.

## Reacquisition: still 31 December 2027, so far as we can confirm

MAECI consular notices state the window in these words:

> *"Le dichiarazioni di riacquisto potranno essere presentate tra il 1° luglio 2025 e il 31 dicembre 2027."*
>
> "Declarations of reacquisition may be lodged between 1 July 2025 and 31 December 2027."

One of the 2026 consular notices is titled in the plural — *proroga termini normativi*, extension of statutory deadlines — which has led some readers to assume the reacquisition window moved too. **The only extension we could confirm is the minors' declaration deadline moving to 31 May 2029.** We are not telling you the 2027 reacquisition deadline was extended, and we are not telling you it was not. Check before you rely on either.

## Does any of this add a language requirement?

Not on the sources we read. Neither the coordinated text of D.L. 36/2025 nor the MAECI consular notices implementing it contains an Italian-language condition for the routes they govern — *iure sanguinis* recognition, minors' declarations, or reacquisition. Two Interior Ministry circolari from 2026 on the minors' deadline likewise say nothing about language, the certifying-body list or the filing portal.

Treat that as what it is: a negative finding on the documents we could open, which is weaker than a positive one. We could not open every article of the law. The B1 requirement under art. 9.1 of Law 91/1992 continues to govern applications under articles 5 and 9, unchanged by anything on this page.

## FAQ

**I filed a consular application before 27 March 2025. Am I affected?**
An application lodged with a consulate or a mayor by 23:59 Rome time on 27 March 2025 is among the conditions the coordinated text specifies. Whether your particular file qualifies is a question for the office holding it.

**My child was born after 24 May 2025. Does the 2029 deadline help?**
The consular notice frames the extended deadline by reference to children who were minors as at 24 May 2025. We have not verified how births after that date are treated.

**Which pages should I check before acting?**
The Gazzetta Ufficiale records, the Normattiva coordinated text and the Ministry circolare, all linked above. Where a consular notice differs from the Gazzetta Ufficiale, the Gazzetta Ufficiale is the record.
