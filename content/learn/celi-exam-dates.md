Miss this and nothing else on the page helps you: the enrolment deadline that binds you is your centre's, and it can fall before the one Perugia publishes. The regulation is explicit that the centre sets its own closing date within the CVCL limit:

> "La scadenza per la presentazione delle domande di iscrizione è regolata dal Centro, che dovrà in ogni caso rispettare la scadenza indicata dal CVCL nel sito www.cvcl.it."
> *The deadline for submitting applications is set by the Centre, which must in any case respect the deadline indicated by CVCL on www.cvcl.it.*
> — Regolamento degli esami CELI, art. 1.5

Read that as a ceiling, not a date. A centre may close registrations before the CVCL cut-off and is entitled to — the regulation fixes only the direction, earlier and never later, and states no interval, so we do not give one. Ask the centre for its own date, in writing, and work to that.

You enrol through an affiliated exam centre — *Centro d'esame convenzionato* — not with Perugia directly, and the centre collects the fee when the application is submitted.

## How the session calendar is built

The regulation sets out the pattern before any specific dates:

> "Due sono le sessioni d'esame CELI complete nel corso dell'anno solare… una sessione nel mese di giugno e una sessione nel mese di novembre."
> *There are two complete CELI exam sessions in the calendar year… one in June and one in November.*
> — Regolamento degli esami CELI, art. 1.3

"Complete" means all six levels of the CELI scale. Around those two, three more patterns run:

- A **March** session covering the first four levels only, A1 to B2.
- One session a year in **May** for CELI Adolescenti.
- **One or more sessions a year** for CELI Immigrati, according to the published calendar.

The regulation also states that exams take place simultaneously at all centres worldwide, time zones permitting. Your sitting is not scheduled locally; the date is fixed centrally.

The practical consequence for a B1 candidate: CELI 2 appears in March, June and November, and the immigrant-series B1 module appears at the immigrant sittings. A candidate needing C1 or C2 has two chances a year.

First, the February 2026 and February 2027 rows print "A2 CELI i", where every other immigrant-series row prints "A2 CELI 1 i". That looks like a typographical slip on the source page, but CVCL has not corrected it and we are not resolving it. If you are booking a February A2 immigrant sitting, confirm the exam code with your centre.

Second, on the calendar as retrieved, the 14 August 2026 deadline for the 16 September 2026 immigrant session had already passed. An observation of the published calendar rather than an official rule: across the fourteen 2026 and 2027 rows above, every CVCL deadline falls between 26 and 35 days before its sitting. A session that looks comfortably distant may already be closed.

## If you cannot attend

The regulation deals with absence in article 1.9, and the provision is more generous than most candidates expect — but it is conditional.

A candidate who cannot sit the exam because of illness must produce a medical certificate justifying the absence. They may then present themselves at the immediately following session, at the same centre, exempt from paying the fee again. The catch is in the next sentence of the article: the candidate must in any case have submitted a regular enrolment application by the closing date. Absence for reasons other than illness, if duly justified, is assessed by CVCL.

Two things follow. The exemption attaches to the *next* session, not to any session you like. And you must have been properly enrolled in the first place — a missed deadline is not a missed exam.

## Questions

**Can I sit CELI 2 at any of the three generic sessions?**
B1 falls within the first four levels, so it appears in March as well as in the two complete June and November sessions. Confirm against the calendar for the year you want.

**Does my centre choose the exam date?**
No. The date is fixed centrally and exams run simultaneously worldwide, time zones permitting. The centre chooses its own enrolment closing date within the CVCL limit.

**How much notice do I need?**
Counting the rows in the calendar above rather than applying any published rule, the CVCL cut-offs fall between 26 and 35 days before their sittings — but that is the CVCL cut-off, and your centre's may be earlier.

**What if I enrol and then fall ill?**
Medical certificate, next session, same centre, no further fee, per article 1.9 — provided your enrolment was properly submitted by the deadline.
