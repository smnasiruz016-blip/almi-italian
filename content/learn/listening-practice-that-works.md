*"Gli esami CILS cominciano con le prove di ascolto"* — CILS exams begin with the listening tests. Unistrasi states it on its exam-conduct page, and it is the most under-used piece of exam-day information the university publishes.

It means the listening happens at the coldest moment of your day: before your ear has adjusted, before you have written a word of Italian, possibly before your coffee has worked. Nothing else in the exam is arranged that way. Practice can be arranged to match it.

## The shape of the CILS B1 Cittadinanza ascolto

Per the syllabus, the ascolto is two tests taking around thirty minutes in total, with 400 to 700 words of audio across them, and the texts are played twice. The two formats are a three-option multiple choice and an information-identification task.

The criteria document sets out how the points fall:

| Test | Format | Items | Value per item | Max |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ascolto, Prova 1 | Multiple choice | 6 | 1 point | 6 |
| Ascolto, Prova 2 | True/false | 12 | 0.5 points | 6 |
| **Ascolto total** | | **18** | | **12** |

Look at the arithmetic before you look at any advice. One multiple-choice item is worth two true/false items. The twelve-item true/false test is where a candidate can bleed slowly — half a point at a time, on items that individually feel too small to fight for — and where a steady, methodical run is worth more than brilliance on any single question.

## Practising for a twice-played text

Because each text is played twice, the two listenings should not do the same job.

- **First play: shape and certainty.** Get the situation, who is speaking, what it is about. Mark only the answers you are sure of, and put a visible mark next to the items you are not.
- **Between plays: aim.** You now know exactly which items are unresolved. Reread just those questions.
- **Second play: hunt.** You are listening for two or three specific things rather than everything at once.

Train this deliberately. Play a text twice, with a real pause between, and forbid yourself from restarting or pausing mid-audio. The single most common way home practice diverges from the exam is the pause button.

Read the questions before the audio starts, whenever the paper gives you a moment to. Knowing what you are being asked converts listening from comprehension into a search.

## Use the official audio

Unistrasi publishes free preparation material for B1 Cittadinanza on the same page as the syllabus and criteria: a prototype exam booklet, the audio file, the transcriptions and the updated answer keys. That is the closest thing to the real item formats that exists, and it costs nothing.

Use the transcript *after* the attempt, never during it. Reading along while listening feels productive and trains a skill the exam does not test. When you do go to the transcript, the useful question is not "what did I miss" but "why did I miss it" — unknown word, unexpected speed, a distractor that matched a word in the question, or attention that drifted at minute two.

## Why a weak ascolto is more expensive at CILS B1 Cittadinanza

The Cittadinanza module does not allow *capitalizzazione* (banking a passed skill for a later session). If the exam is not passed in some skill, the whole exam is retaken at the next session — Unistrasi says so explicitly, and an Italian government information sheet repeats it. So the listening you sit at nine in the morning cannot be repaired later that day or carried over to a later sitting on its own.

Be careful about how far you take that reasoning, though. Siena's criteria document scores each *abilità* out of {{CILS_B1C_SECTION_MAX}} separately, but Siena publishes **no** per-skill minimum and no overall pass mark for this module. Our practice tool uses a {{CILS_B1C_FLOOR}}/{{CILS_B1C_SECTION_MAX}} floor per skill and a {{CILS_B1C_TOTAL_FLOOR}}/{{CILS_B1C_TOTAL_MAX}} total; those are our numbers, derived from the official standard CILS UNO–B1 rule ({{CILS_STANDARD_TOTAL_MAX}} points, 55 to pass, minimum {{CILS_STANDARD_FLOOR}} per skill — 55 per cent, or 6.6 of 12, rounded to 7; 4 × 7 = 28). Anyone quoting you an official per-skill minimum for B1 Cittadinanza is quoting something Siena has not published.

## CELI puts listening somewhere else entirely

At Perugia, listening is not a free-standing passable unit. CVCL's regulation defines the *parte scritta* as containing reading comprehension, written production and listening comprehension together — and it is that whole written part that has to reach its minimum, alongside a separately-passed oral.

For CELI 2 (B1), the listening paper runs twenty minutes: three tasks, 33 items, two points per correct answer, up to 40 points, which then sit inside a written-part total of 120. For the CELI 2 i (B1) Cittadinanza module the listening is four tasks, also twenty minutes, including binary-choice tasks of twelve and eight items.

The practical consequence is a genuine structural difference, and it is the only one we will state: at CELI, a weaker listening is aggregated with reading and writing before any threshold is applied, whereas at CILS the ascolto is scored as its own *abilità* out of {{CILS_B1C_SECTION_MAX}} — with the important caveat that Siena has not published what, if anything, that score has to reach on its own. What nobody official has published is any comparison of the two exams' difficulty, in either direction.

## Three honest answers

**Is the audio played twice at CELI as well?**
The CILS syllabus states it for B1 Cittadinanza. We have not found a CVCL statement either way for CELI, so we are not going to tell you. Ask your exam centre.

**How much listening am I actually being asked to process?**
For CILS B1 Cittadinanza, 400 to 700 words of audio across the two tests, in about thirty minutes including the time you spend on the paper. That is less continuous audio than most people imagine.

**Should I practise with films, songs and podcasts?**
They will help your ear and your vocabulary and they cost nothing, so yes — but they are not the exam. The exam is short recorded texts followed by 3-option multiple choice and true/false items, and the item formats have to be practised as item formats.
