Italian law sets a level, not an exam name. The statute requires knowledge of Italian not below B1 and a certificate from a recognised certifying body; it does not name modules. Perugia, meanwhile, publishes a B1 exam whose official name ends with the word *cittadinanza*. Holding both facts at once is the whole difficulty of this page.

## What the series contains

CVCL describes the CELI Immigrati family as covering three CEFR levels, A1 to B1, and as aimed at:

> "cittadini stranieri legati a un progetto migratorio stanziale"
> *foreign citizens tied to a settled migration project.*
> — CVCL, Esami CELI Immigrati

The three exams, as the official calendar prints them, carry the suffix **i**: CELI Impatto i at A1, CELI 1 i at A2, and CELI 2 i at B1, the last of which the calendar writes out as "B1 CELI 2 i cittadinanza". The exam description PDF for that module is titled "CELI 2 i (B1) - Cittadinanza".

CVCL's immigrant-series page also connects CELI 1 i (A2) with the *permesso di soggiorno UE per soggiornanti di lungo periodo* (EU long-term residence permit). We obtained that connection only as a summary of the page rather than as printed text, and it is a statement by the exam provider, not by a ministry. Whether any particular certificate relieves you of the state A2 test is a matter for the Ministry of the Interior; read its page on the *test di conoscenza della lingua italiana* and confirm there before you rely on it.

## CELI 2 i is not CELI 2 with a suffix

The citizenship module has its own structure, and it differs from the generic CELI 2 in ways that change how you should prepare.

| Part | CELI 2 i (B1) Cittadinanza | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Prova A — Comprensione della Lettura | 4 tasks across 5 texts | Three-option multiple choice, information identification, multiple-choice completion |
| Prova B — Produzione ed Interazione scritta | 3 tasks | A formal e-mail of 40 to 50 words; an informal message of 80 to 90 words |
| Prova C — Comprensione dell'Ascolto | 4 tasks, 20 minutes | Binary choice, one task of 12 items and one of 8 |
| Prova orale | 5 components, 10 to 12 minutes | Self-presentation, image description, intercultural mediation, civic topics, dialogue |

Note the name of the writing paper. At CELI 2 i it is *Produzione ed Interazione scritta* — written production **and interaction** — not *Produzione di Testi Scritti* as in the generic exam. The task types follow that: two pieces of real correspondence with tight word limits rather than one longer letter.

The published total duration is stated plainly:

> "Durata totale dell'esame: 2 ore e 30 minuti"
> *Total duration of the exam: 2 hours and 30 minutes.*
> — CELI 2 i (B1) Cittadinanza, descrizione esame

The four parts are described as carrying equal weight, a quarter each. We obtained that weighting as a summary rather than as printed text.

## The thresholds — and the two-point difference

The criteria document for CELI 2 i states a written-part minimum of {{CELI_DUE_WRITTEN_MIN}}, an oral minimum of 11 raw points doubled to 22, and an overall minimum of 92 out of 160. Component maxima are 40 for each of Parts A, B and C, giving a written part of 120, plus an oral of 20 doubled to 40.

Here is a contradiction we are not going to resolve for you, because the official documents do not resolve it either:

| | CELI 2 (generico, B1) | CELI 2 i (B1) Cittadinanza |
|---|---|---|
| Written part minimum | 72 | 72 |
| Oral minimum | 22 | 22 |
| **Overall minimum** | **94** | **92** |
| Total available | 160 | 160 |

Two B1 exams, both out of 160, both requiring 72 and 22, with overall cut-offs two points apart. The most likely reading is that these are simply two different exams with separately set aggregate thresholds, but no official document says so. Each number belongs to its own exam. Do not average them and do not carry one across.

A second unresolved point: the CELI 2 i criteria document prints **no** A/B/C/D/E bands, only numeric ranges per section, even though the Regolamento's general rule reserves ungraded pass/fail results for A1 and A2. We confirmed that absence on two separate readings of the document. So we cannot tell you whether a CELI 2 i pass arrives with a letter grade, and we will not guess.

## The citizenship question, stated carefully

The Ministry of the Interior's list of *enti certificatori* (certifying bodies) is the only authority on what counts. The Università per Stranieri di Perugia appears on that list, with CELI as its certification. That much is on the record in government documents.

What is **not** on the record anywhere we could find: any government statement that only the *cittadinanza* module counts, and any CVCL statement that a generic CELI 2 does or does not count. The law speaks of a level and a body. The exam provider publishes a module named for citizenship. Nobody official joins those two facts up.

So: we cannot tell you that a generic CELI 2 will be accepted, and we cannot tell you it will be refused. Before booking, ask your Prefettura, and write to certificazione@unistrapg.it. Get the answer in writing from the office that will process your file.

## When the immigrant sessions run

The Regolamento says CELI Immigrati has one or more sessions a year according to the published calendar, separate from the June and November generic sessions. In 2026 the calendar lists immigrant sittings on 18 February, 6 May and 16 September; in 2027, on 17 February, 20 May and 15 September. Not every level appears at every sitting, and calendars change — read the calendar page rather than this paragraph when you book.

## Questions

**Is CELI 2 i easier than CELI 2 because it is shorter?**
We do not make that claim about any exam pairing. The documents describe different tasks and different aggregate cut-offs; they do not rank difficulty.

**Can I bank a passed part from a generic CELI 2 into a CELI 2 i sitting?**
The Regolamento's *capitalizzazione* article requires the re-sit to be at the same level **and** the same exam type. On the plain reading of that wording, a generic partial does not carry into a citizenship sitting. No source confirms the mechanism operates for the citizenship module at all.

**Does a CELI 1 i (A2) pass exempt me from the state A2 test?**
Not a question we can answer. The Ministry decides who is *non tenuto* to sit the test; check its page and ask the Prefettura.
