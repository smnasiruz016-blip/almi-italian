Four tests, two completely different kinds of marking. That is the fact most practice plans ignore, and it is the reason a candidate can be well drilled and still lose points where it hurts.

In CILS B1 Cittadinanza, two of the four *abilità* (skills) are counted. **Ascolto** is two prove worth 6 points each: a six-item multiple-choice test at one point per item, and a twelve-item true/false test at half a point per item. **Comprensione della lettura e riflessione grammaticale** is built the same way — a twelve-item true/false test and a six-item multiple-choice cloze, 6 points each. An answer is right or it is not; nobody forms a judgement about you.

The other two are *produttive*. **Produzione scritta** and **produzione orale** are worth 12 points each, and those 12 points are broken into named sub-criteria that Siena publishes. That is what you should be practising against.

## The published weights

Both tables below come from the Unistrasi criteria document for B1 Cittadinanza. They are official and quotable; they belong to CILS and to no other exam.

| Produzione scritta (max 12) | Points |
|---|---|
| Efficacia comunicativa | 3 |
| Adeguatezza stilistica | 1 |
| Correttezza morfosintattica | 4 |
| Adeguatezza e ricchezza lessicale | 3 |
| Ortografia e punteggiatura | 1 |

| Produzione orale (max 12) | Points |
|---|---|
| Efficacia comunicativa | 4 |
| Correttezza morfosintattica | 4 |
| Adeguatezza e ricchezza lessicale | 3 |
| Pronuncia e intonazione | 1 |

Read them side by side and two things jump out. *Correttezza morfosintattica* (grammatical and syntactic accuracy) carries 4 points in both — the heaviest single band in either task. And *efficacia comunicativa* (communicative effectiveness: did you actually do what the task asked?) is worth 3 in writing but 4 in speaking. Same name, different weight. A practice plan that treats the two tasks as one thing gets that wrong.

## A loop that matches the marking

Marking is centralised. Unistrasi's own page states: *"Tutte le prove di esame sono valutate centralmente presso il Centro CILS, che ha elaborato i criteri per la valutazione"* — all exam papers are assessed centrally at the CILS Centre, which drew up the assessment criteria. Nobody at your exam centre decides your score, and no teacher's personal taste is the standard. The criteria list is.

So practise like this:

1. **Do the task under its real constraint.** For the written task that means 40 minutes and {{CILS_B1C_SCRITTA_MIN_WORDS}}–{{CILS_B1C_SCRITTA_MAX_WORDS}} words, on an email that either requests information or resolves a problem with a service. For the oral, roughly a one-minute personal presentation followed by a two-to-three-minute dialogue.
2. **Mark yourself criterion by criterion**, out of the published maxima above — not out of a general impression.
3. **Pick the one criterion you lost most on** and change one habit, rather than trying to fix everything.
4. **Redo the same prompt** a few days later. Repeating a prompt is not cheating; it is how you find out whether the habit changed.

Unistrasi publishes free preparation material on its B1 Cittadinanza page, including the syllabus, a prototype exam booklet, the audio file, the transcriptions, the answer keys and the criteria document itself. Start there before you start anywhere else.

## Where our numbers stop being Siena's

Our practice tool shows a {{CILS_B1C_FLOOR}}/{{CILS_B1C_SECTION_MAX}} floor per skill and a total of {{CILS_B1C_TOTAL_FLOOR}}/{{CILS_B1C_TOTAL_MAX}}. Those thresholds are ours, not Siena's: we derived them from the official standard CILS UNO–B1 rule ({{CILS_STANDARD_TOTAL_MAX}} points, 55 to pass, minimum {{CILS_STANDARD_FLOOR}} per skill, which is 55 per cent, or 6.6 out of {{CILS_B1C_SECTION_MAX}}, rounded to 7 — and 4 × 7 = 28). Siena publishes **no** pass mark and no per-section minimum for the Cittadinanza module. If you see a specific "you need X out of {{CILS_B1C_TOTAL_MAX}}" figure anywhere, ask who published it.

There is also an unresolved conflict worth knowing about: Siena's own criteria document for this module totals {{CILS_B1C_TOTAL_MAX}} points, while an Italian government information sheet describes both B1 exams as scored out of 100 with 55 to pass and a minimum of {{CILS_STANDARD_FLOOR}} per skill. No official document explains how one maps onto the other. We report both and resolve neither.

## CELI works differently — keep it separate

If you are sitting a CELI exam at Perugia instead, none of the numbers above apply to you. CVCL groups reading, writing and listening inside a single *parte scritta* and scores the oral as a separate passable part, with its own published scales. In CELI 2 (B1), the letter or e-mail task is scored from 0 to 20 points; in the CELI 2 i cittadinanza module, the three writing tasks are scored 0–12, 0–15 and 0–20. Those are Perugia's numbers for Perugia's exam.

No official source — not CVCL, not Unistrasi, not the Ministry of the Interior — publishes a comparison of the two marking systems. Do not import CILS criteria weights into CELI practice, and do not let anyone tell you which exam marks more generously; nobody official has said.

## Questions people actually ask

**Should I rehearse the whole exam in one sitting?**
For CILS B1 Cittadinanza, yes, and the reason is structural. Unistrasi states plainly: *"Per questo livello non è consentita la 'capitalizzazione'"* — for this level, banking a passed skill is not allowed. Fail one skill and the whole exam is retaken at the next session, so stamina across roughly two hours and fifteen minutes is part of what you are training.

**Can I have my paper re-marked to learn from my mistakes?**
No official re-mark or appeal procedure is published. What Unistrasi does publish is a right to view your papers within 30 days of results, by writing to trasparenzacertificazioni@unistrasi.it with your ID, your details and your matricola number, and paying the access fee under Law 241/90. Viewing is not re-marking.

**If I score well in practice, will I pass?**
No practice score predicts an official result, ours included. Practice tells you which criterion you are weakest on. That is genuinely useful, and it is a different thing from a forecast.
