Start with the contradiction, because it is the first thing an honest scoring page has to declare. Università per Stranieri di Siena's own assessment-criteria document for CILS B1 Cittadinanza adds up to **{{CILS_B1C_TOTAL_MAX}} points**. An Italian government information sheet published by an Italian Cultural Institute says that for both B1 exams the maximum is **{{CILS_STANDARD_TOTAL_MAX}} points with a minimum of 55**, each skill marked out of {{CILS_STANDARD_SECTION_MAX}} with a minimum of {{CILS_STANDARD_FLOOR}}. Siena's general assessment page, meanwhile, describes B1 out of 100 and never mentions the Cittadinanza module at all.

One reading is that raw marks out of {{CILS_B1C_TOTAL_MAX}} are rescaled onto a 100-point reporting scale. **No official document says so**, and we are not going to say it for them. Below, every number stays inside the system that published it.

## CILS — Università per Stranieri di Siena

### B1 Cittadinanza: what the criteria document publishes

| Test | Format as printed | Maximum |
|---|---|---|
| Ascolto, prova 1 | Multiple choice, 6 items | 6 points (1 per item) |
| Ascolto, prova 2 | True/false, 12 items | 6 points (0.5 per item) |
| Lettura, prova 1 | True/false, 12 items | 6 points (0.5 per item) |
| Lettura, prova 2 | Multiple-choice completion, 6 items | 6 points |
| Produzione scritta | *Prova a tema*, {{CILS_B1C_SCRITTA_MIN_WORDS}}–{{CILS_B1C_SCRITTA_MAX_WORDS}} words | 12 points |
| Produzione orale | *Prova a tema* | 12 points |

That is 12 points per abilità across four abilità, and 48 in total.

The two productive tasks are broken into weighted criteria, and the weights are published.

| Criterion | Produzione scritta | Produzione orale |
|---|---|---|
| Efficacia comunicativa (communicative effectiveness) | 3 | 4 |
| Correttezza morfosintattica (morphosyntactic accuracy) | 4 | 4 |
| Adeguatezza e ricchezza lessicale (lexical range and appropriateness) | 3 | 3 |
| Adeguatezza stilistica (stylistic appropriateness) | 1 | — |
| Ortografia e punteggiatura (spelling and punctuation) | 1 | — |
| Pronuncia e intonazione (pronunciation and intonation) | — | 1 |

The criteria document expresses each of these as a ceiling — for example *"efficacia comunicativa: fino a punti 3"*, up to 3 points.

### The number Siena does not publish

**There is no official pass mark, and no official per-section minimum, for the B1 Cittadinanza module.** We looked for one in the criteria document, the syllabus, the sample paper, the assessment page and the FAQ. Any specific figure you meet elsewhere — a school's page, a video — is very unlikely to trace back to Unistrasi.

Our own practice tool shows a floor of {{CILS_B1C_FLOOR}} out of {{CILS_B1C_SECTION_MAX}} per skill and a total of {{CILS_B1C_TOTAL_FLOOR}} out of {{CILS_B1C_TOTAL_MAX}}. **Those thresholds are ours, not Siena's:** we derived them from the standard B1 rule below ({{CILS_STANDARD_FLOOR}} out of {{CILS_STANDARD_SECTION_MAX}} is 55%, which is 6.6 out of {{CILS_B1C_SECTION_MAX}}, rounded to 7; four skills at 7 gives 28). Treat them as a practice target, never as the official bar.

### Standard CILS UNO–B1, which does publish thresholds

For the standard exam — a different, longer paper — Unistrasi's *Linee guida* set five abilità at 20 points each for a maximum of 100, with **55 to pass** and a **minimum of {{CILS_STANDARD_FLOOR}} in each skill**. This is the rule the government sheet appears to be describing when it quotes 100 and 55 for "both" B1 exams.

Marking for all CILS papers is central: the Centro CILS in Siena assesses the scripts and wrote the criteria.

## CELI — CVCL, Università per Stranieri di Perugia

CELI is built differently, and the difference is architectural rather than cosmetic. The *Regolamento degli esami CELI* treats each exam as two passable units: the *parte scritta*, which contains reading, writing and listening together, and the *parte orale*. A candidate passes only by reaching the minimum in both. Reaching the overall total while failing one part is not a pass.

The final result is the sum of the individual tests, converted into five letter grades: A (ottimo), B (buono) and C (sufficiente) are passes; D (insufficiente) and E (gravemente insufficiente) are fails. At A1 and A2 there is no letter grading at all — the result is communicated simply as positive or negative.

### CELI 2 (B1), generic

| Unit | Maximum |
|---|---|
| Parte A — Comprensione della lettura | 40 |
| Parte B — Produzione scritta | 40 |
| Parte C — Comprensione dell'ascolto | 40 |
| **Prova scritta total** | **120** |
| Prova di produzione orale | 40 |
| **Exam total** | **160** |

Reading and listening items are worth two points for each correct answer; the three writing tasks are marked 0–5, 0–15 and 0–20; the oral is marked 0–20 and doubled. The minimum is 72 in the written part and 22 in the oral, and the criteria document states: *"L'esame è considerato superato se il punteggio ottenuto è compreso tra 94 e 160."* (*The exam is considered passed if the score obtained falls between 94 and 160.*)

| Score | Giudizio |
|---|---|
| 138–160 | A = ottimo |
| 115–137 | B = buono |
| 94–114 | C = sufficiente |
| 60–93 | D = insufficiente |
| 0–59 | E = gravemente insufficiente |

### CELI 2 i (B1) Cittadinanza

The citizenship module is scored on its own document, and two things differ. The written minimum is again 72 and the oral minimum is 11 raw, doubled to 22 — but the overall minimum is **92, not 94**. And the CELI 2 i criteria document publishes no A/B/C/D/E bands at all, even though the Regolamento's general rule reserves ungraded pass/fail for A1 and A2 only.

Both are B1 exams; both total 160; both require 72 and 22. Yet the aggregate cut-offs differ by two points, and one document has bands where the other does not. We report both and resolve neither. Quote each number against its own exam.

## Frequently asked

**Can I convert a CILS score into a CELI grade?**
No. 48 and 160 are separate scales belonging to separate institutions, and no official source maps one onto the other — or compares them in any way.

**Is there an appeal if I disagree with a mark?**
No formal re-mark procedure is published by either body. Unistrasi allows candidates to ask to view their papers within 30 days of publication under Law 241/90; CVCL provides for requests for clarification and statutory access to records. Those are not re-marks.

**Why does our tool show {{CILS_B1C_TOTAL_FLOOR}}/{{CILS_B1C_TOTAL_MAX}} if that is not official?**
Because a practice tool has to draw a line somewhere, and we would rather show you our arithmetic than borrow a number no institution stands behind.
