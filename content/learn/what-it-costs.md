One number on this page comes from an awarding body. The rest of this page is about why the other numbers you will find online do not.

## The one official figure

Università per Stranieri di Siena publishes the fee for its citizenship module on the exam's own page:

> "La tassa di esame è di 100 euro, in Italia e all'estero."
>
> *The exam fee is 100 euros, in Italy and abroad.* — Unistrasi, page for the CILS B1 Cittadinanza exam

Two qualifications belong with it. It is a point-in-time figure: fees change, and we recorded this one in August 2026. And it applies to that module only. Unistrasi publishes no equivalent figure for A1, A2, the standard UNO–B1, B2, C1 or C2 on any page we could reach.

## How CILS fees are actually set

The manual Unistrasi gives its exam venues says the venue must ask each candidate for the fee for the chosen level according to what is established in the convention or protocol of agreement between the university and that venue. The candidate-facing registration page gives no figures at all; it tells you to contact the venue.

So the widely repeated claim that "each centre sets its own price" is **not supported by the official wording**. The safer description is: the university sets the schedule through its conventions, the centre collects on the university's behalf, and for B1 Cittadinanza the university publicly quotes €100.

There is a document circulating off-site that appears to be a standard Unistrasi convention and that lists a range of per-level fees. It is undated, it conflicts with the €100 figure, and we are not going to reprint its numbers or let you plan around them. If you need the fee for a level other than B1 Cittadinanza, ask the centre and get it in writing.

## CELI: no published price, and that is the finding

CVCL publishes no euro amount for any CELI exam. What it publishes is the mechanism.

The fee is collected by the centre at the moment the enrolment application is submitted. Its amount is governed by the conventions CVCL signs with individual venues, includes Italian stamp duty at the ordinary rate, and is owed entirely to CVCL. And the centre has an obligation you can hold it to:

> "il Centro è tenuto a specificare il prezzo unitario della tassa di iscrizione all'esame CELI, scorporato da eventuali oneri aggiuntivi relativi a servizi potenzialmente offerti quali, a titolo esemplificativo, corsi di preparazione all'esame o costi di segreteria."
>
> *the Centre must specify the unit price of the CELI exam enrolment fee, separated from any additional charges for services potentially offered such as, by way of example, exam preparation courses or administrative costs.* — Regolamento degli esami CELI, art. 1.6

That sentence is the most useful thing on this page for a CELI candidate. If a quote arrives as one bundled figure covering a course and the exam, you are entitled to see the exam fee on its own line. CVCL's enrolment page adds that it is the centre's responsibility to tell candidates the amount and the payment methods.

Amounts for CELI circulate on school and adult-education sites. Every one of them is a centre-level price, not an official rate, and we will not repeat any of them.

The only price-adjacent figure CVCL itself publishes is a percentage: a candidate re-sitting one part under capitalizzazione pays **70% of the full fee**. Note that we could not confirm whether that mechanism operates for the *immigrati* and cittadinanza modules at all.

CVCL also lists a refund regulation — "Regolamento per la gestione dei rimborsi", issued by rectoral decree in April 2026. We confirmed the document's title but not its contents, so if you need to know whether a fee is refundable, read that regulation or ask certificazione@unistrapg.it rather than trusting a summary.

## Costs beyond the exam fee

For a citizenship application, the exam fee is not the largest line. A state contribution is payable under art. 9-bis of Legge 5 febbraio 1992, n. 91 on applications filed from 5 October 2018, and the receipt for it is one of the documents the electronic application must carry. We are not printing an amount here: the figure we could source dates from a 2019 consular notice, and money that decides whether a filing is accepted deserves better than a seven-year-old citation. Check the current official page before you pay.

Two smaller costs are genuinely local. Consulates charge for a certified copy (*copia conforme*) of the language certificate during verification, and consular tariffs vary by post — ask yours. Unistrasi charges an access fee, under Law 241/90, to candidates who ask to view their exam papers within 30 days of publication, but does not publish the amount.

## Frequently asked

**Does the €100 cover a re-sit if I fail?**
No official text says either way. Since CILS B1 Cittadinanza does not allow capitalizzazione — a failed skill means sitting the whole exam again at a later session — assume you are enrolling and paying afresh, and ask the centre to confirm.

**Is CELI cheaper or more expensive than CILS?**
Unanswerable from official sources, because CVCL publishes no figure to compare. Anyone presenting a side-by-side price table is comparing one official number to an unofficial one.

**My centre wants payment for a preparation course together with the exam.**
For CELI, the regulation requires the exam fee to be itemised separately from preparation courses and administrative charges. Ask for that breakdown.

**Why won't you just publish a fee table?**
Because the only figures available for most levels come from third parties, and a reader who books a session around a wrong number loses both the money and the sitting. One official number, clearly labelled, is worth more than twelve plausible ones.
