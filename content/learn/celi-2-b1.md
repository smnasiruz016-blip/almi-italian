Two hours, then twenty minutes, then ten to twelve minutes with two examiners. That is the whole shape of CELI 2, the generic B1 exam in the CELI scale, as set out in CVCL's published description of the test.

Before anything else, one boundary: this page describes **CELI 2**, the exam in the generic series. There is a separate B1 exam in the immigrant series, calendared as CELI 2 i cittadinanza, and its published thresholds are not identical. Every number below belongs to CELI 2 generico and to nothing else.

## The four parts and what is in them

The written exam is delivered in two booklets, *fascicoli*. Booklet 1 holds Parts A and B; Booklet 2 holds Part C, the listening.

| Part | Official name | Tasks | Items | Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Prova di Comprensione della lettura | 5 | 37 | Booklet 1: 2 hours (with B) |
| B | Prova di Produzione di Testi Scritti | 3 | 11 | Booklet 1: 2 hours (with A) |
| C | Prova di Comprensione dell'ascolto | 3 | 33 | 20 minutes |
| — | Prova di Produzione Orale | 3 components | — | About 10 to 12 minutes |

The naming is worth noticing: the exam description PDF calls the writing paper *Produzione di Testi Scritti*, while the scoring PDF for the same exam calls it *Produzione scritta*. Both are in official use and refer to the same paper.

### Part A — reading

Five tasks. A.1 is short texts with three-option multiple choice (7 items). A.2 is one text with ten binary-choice matches (10 items). A.3 is sentences to complete with four-option multiple choice (5 items). A.4 is one text with ten three-option cloze completions (10 items). A.5 is sentences to complete (5 items).

### Part B — writing

Three tasks. B.1 is a form or questionnaire to answer on general-interest topics (9 items). B.2 is a short announcement — the description gives a length of roughly 50 words, but we could only obtain that figure as a summary rather than as printed text, so treat it as approximate. B.3 is the substantial task:

> "una breve lettera o e-mail da scrivere"
> *a short letter or e-mail to write.*
> — CELI 2, descrizione della prova

The stated length for B.3 is 90 to 100 words. That figure, too, came back as extracted text rather than as a verbatim string, so confirm it against the PDF before you build a practice routine around it.

### Part C — listening

Three tasks. C.1 and C.2 are short messages or news items with three-option multiple choice, four items each. C.3 is two texts with binary-choice matching, 25 items. Twenty minutes for the booklet.

### The oral

A face-to-face interview between one candidate and a commission of two examiners. It has three components: an interview or conversation with the interlocutor, a photograph or image to describe, and a role play — a communicative task inside a clearly defined situation. Materials are given to the candidate around twelve minutes before the test begins, though we obtained that detail as a summary rather than as printed text.

The commission assesses on the spot and does not tell the candidate the score.

## No Competenza Linguistica component at this level

The Regolamento defines the written part as reading comprehension, written production, *Competenza Linguistica* — explicitly from CELI 3 upwards — and listening comprehension. At CELI 2 the linguistic-competence component therefore does not appear. If you have seen a CELI structure that includes it, you were looking at B2 or above.

## The {{CELI_DUE_TOTAL_MAX}}-point scale

The criteria and scores document sets out item-level values and section maxima.

| Unit | Maximum | How points are awarded |
|---|---|---|
| Part A — reading | 40 | 2 points per correct answer across A.1 to A.5 |
| Part B — writing | 40 | B.1: 0 to 5 · B.2: 0 to 15 · B.3: 0 to 20 |
| Part C — listening | 40 | 2 points per correct answer across C.1 to C.3 |
| **Written part (A+B+C)** | **120** | |
| Oral | 40 | 0 to 20 on the competence scales, doubled |
| **Exam total** | **160** | |

Two honest caveats. The item-level values above are printed in the document; the arithmetic that converts the raw item totals in Parts A and C onto a 40-point scale is not spelled out in the material we could retrieve. And marking of the written part is centralised at CVCL, carried out by examiners trained for the task — your local centre does not mark it.

## The thresholds

CELI is passed only if the minimum is reached in **both** the written part and the oral part. For CELI 2 the document states a minimum of {{CELI_DUE_WRITTEN_MIN}} points in the written test and {{CELI_DUE_ORAL_MIN}} points in the oral test, and for the exam as a whole:

> "L'esame è considerato superato se il punteggio ottenuto è compreso tra 94 e 160."
> *The exam is considered passed if the score obtained falls between 94 and 160.*
> — CELI 2, criteri di valutazione e punteggi

Both conditions bind. Ninety-four points assembled with an oral below {{CELI_DUE_ORAL_MIN}} is not a pass.

### The letter bands for CELI 2

| Score | Giudizio |
|---|---|
| 138–160 | A = ottimo |
| 115–137 | B = buono |
| 94–114 | C = sufficiente |
| 60–93 | D = insufficiente |
| 0–59 | E = gravemente insufficiente |

## On the day

The Regolamento requires each exam level to be administered in a dedicated room; several levels cannot be run in the same room. Other exam-room rules are described in the same articles — identification, permitted pens, spacing between candidates — but we could not obtain them as printed text, so ask your centre for its instructions rather than relying on a second-hand list.

## Questions

**Can I retake just the listening?**
On the plain reading of the Regolamento the unit is the whole *parte scritta*, which contains reading, writing and listening together. No official source states that as an explicit prohibition, but nothing supports retaking a single component either.

**Is 94 the pass mark for every B1 CELI?**
No. The B1 exam in the immigrant series publishes a different overall figure. Use the number that belongs to the exam you are sitting.

**What if I fail only the oral?**
That is what *capitalizzazione* is for, within limits the Regolamento sets. It has its own page.
