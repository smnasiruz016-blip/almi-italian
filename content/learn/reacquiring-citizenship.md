On 1 March 2026 a new law came into force that moved one Italian citizenship deadline by three years. If you last read about these deadlines in 2025 — including on an earlier version of this page — one of the dates you are holding is out of date, and one is not. This page separates them.

## What changed, and who says so

The instrument is **Legge 27 febbraio 2026, n. 26**, which converted, with modifications, **decreto-legge 31 dicembre 2025, n. 200** on urgent provisions concerning statutory deadlines. It was published in the *Gazzetta Ufficiale*, Serie Generale n. 49, on 28 February 2026, and the Gazzetta records its entry into force as 1 March 2026.

What it moved: the deadline for the **dichiarazione (declaration)** by which citizenship is acquired *per beneficio di legge* in favour of the minor children of Italian citizens — the declaration under art. 4, comma 1-bis, lett. b) of Legge 91/1992. The 2025 reform had originally set that deadline at 31 May 2026. It is now **31 May 2029**.

The Consolato d'Italia in Marseille, publishing in March 2026, states the new date and identifies the people it concerns as *"I genitori italiani di figli minori al 24 maggio 2025"* — "Italian parents of children who were minors as at 24 May 2025". The Consolato in Philadelphia describes the mechanism: articolo 1, comma 19-ter of the conversion law amends articolo 1, comma 1-ter of D.L. 36/2025.

The Ministero dell'Interno has circularised the change: **Circolare n. 19951 of 12 March 2026**, on "Cittadinanza per beneficio di legge in favore di figli minori di cittadini italiani per nascita. Proroga termine dichiarazioni", citing Legge 27 febbraio 2026, n. 26.

So: **31 May 2029 is a deadline for lodging the minors' declaration.** It is not a deadline for anything else.

## What did not visibly change: the riacquisto window

Reacquisition of citizenship — **riacquisto**, under art. 17 of Legge 91/1992 — runs on its own clock. The Consolato Generale d'Italia in Buenos Aires publishes the window in one sentence:

> *"Le dichiarazioni di riacquisto potranno essere presentate tra il 1° luglio 2025 e il 31 dicembre 2027."*
> "Declarations of reacquisition may be submitted between 1 July 2025 and 31 December 2027."

The same post describes who may use it: former Italian citizens born in Italy, or resident in Italy for at least two continuous years, who lost citizenship by 15 August 1992; people who renounced after 16 August 1992 are excluded. The Consolato in Rio de Janeiro corroborates the window. A Consolato in São Paulo notice implementing Legge 74/2025 states the 31 December 2027 date and adds that detailed operating instructions would follow, with procedural priority given to the shorter minors' deadline.

**31 December 2027 is therefore a deadline for filing a dichiarazione di riacquisto**, not for completing the process.

## The honest gap: was the 2027 window extended too?

One of the March 2026 consular notices is titled "Proroga termini normativi riguardanti la cittadinanza italiana" — *deadlines*, plural. That plural is suggestive. But the only extension our research could confirm on an official source is the minors' declaration moving to 31 May 2029.

We therefore will not tell you that the 31 December 2027 reacquisition deadline was extended, and we will not tell you that it was not. If you are planning a riacquisto filing, treat 31 December 2027 as the date and confirm it with your Consolato before you rely on it.

## Two dating discrepancies worth knowing about

**The law's own date.** The Gazzetta Ufficiale and the Ministry's circolare page both say *Legge 27 febbraio 2026, n. 26*. Several MAECI consular notices say *Legge 28 febbraio 2026, n. 26* — which appears to be the publication date. Cite 27 February 2026, n. 26, published 28 February 2026, in force 1 March 2026, and do not be alarmed if your consulate's page says 28.

**The converted decree's date.** The Gazzetta Ufficiale and the Ministry circolare say *decreto-legge 31 dicembre 2025, n. 200*. A Normattiva index title returned in search results reads *1 dicembre 2025, n. 200*. We prefer the Gazzetta's date; the discrepancy sits on Normattiva's own listing and we are not resolving it here.

## Is there a language requirement for reacquisition?

Neither consular notice on riacquisto mentions any Italian-language condition. That is a silence, not a statement. We could not retrieve the current amended text of art. 17 of Legge 91/1992 itself from Normattiva or the Gazzetta Ufficiale, so we cannot tell you that reacquisition carries no language test as settled law. Ask your Consolato.

The B1 requirement we do document elsewhere on this site attaches to art. 5 (marriage or civil union) and art. 9 (residence) — those are the two routes named in art. 9.1 of Legge 91/1992.

## FAQ

**Does the 31 May 2029 extension help me if my child turned 18 after 24 May 2025?**
The Marseille notice frames the beneficiaries as Italian parents of children who were minors as at 24 May 2025. Beyond that framing, we have no verified detail on edge cases. Ask your Consolato.

**Does the 2025 reform itself impose a language test?**
On the sources we could read — the consolidated text of D.L. 36/2025 and the consular notices implementing it — no language condition appears for the routes it governs. That is a negative finding on those documents, and a negative finding is weaker than a positive one.

**Where do I file a riacquisto declaration?**
Our verified sources for the window are consular pages, which address people resident abroad. We have no verified procedure to publish beyond that. Check the current official page for your Consolato.

**Why does an older version of this article say 31 May 2026 for the minors' deadline?**
Because it predates Legge 27 febbraio 2026, n. 26. The minors' declaration deadline was 31 May 2026 as originally enacted in 2025, and it is now 31 May 2029. That correction concerns the minors' declaration and nothing else — in particular it does not concern the riacquisto window, whose position after L. 26/2026 we do not state either way. If you are holding 31 December 2027 for a reacquisition filing, that is a different deadline and this extension says nothing about it.
