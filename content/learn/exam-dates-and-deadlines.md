19 February 2026 was a CILS sitting; enrolment for it closed on 19 January. That gap — roughly a month, sometimes six weeks — is the part candidates miss. The exam date is easy to find; the date that decides whether you sit is the enrolment cut-off, and it has usually passed by the time someone starts looking.

Both calendars below are as published by the awarding bodies. **Calendars change.** Before committing money or a filing plan, open the official page and check the row you are relying on.

## CILS — Università per Stranieri di Siena

Six sessions a year, and the citizenship module (the calendar's **B1 C**) is offered at every one of them.

### 2026

| Exam date | Levels offered | Enrol by |
|---|---|---|
| 19 February | A2 integrazione, B1 C | 19 January |
| 14 April | A2 standard, A2 integrazione, B1 A, B1 C, B2 standard, B2 adolescenti, C1 | 14 March |
| 16 June | All levels | 7 May |
| 22 July | A2 integrazione, B1 C | 22 June |
| 21 October | A2 standard, A2 integrazione, B1 C | 21 September |
| 3 December | All levels | 24 October |

Unistrasi prints these as instructions, one per session: *"Per la sessione del 19 febbraio, iscriviti entro il 19 gennaio."*

### 2027

| Exam date | Enrol by |
|---|---|
| 18 February | 18 January |
| 8 April | 8 March |
| 10 June | 1 May |
| 22 July | 22 June |
| 21 October | 21 September |
| 2 December | 23 October |

B1 C appears at all six 2027 sessions.

An observation rather than an official rule: the cut-off falls about a month before the sitting, except for June (about six weeks) and December (five to six weeks). Both published years follow that shape; do not treat it as a promise about a year not yet published.

## CELI — CVCL, Università per Stranieri di Perugia

The CELI year is built from rules in the Regolamento: two complete sessions covering all six levels, in June and November; a further session in March for the first four levels, A1 to B2; one session a year in May for CELI Adolescenti; and one or more sessions a year for CELI Immigrati according to the published calendar. Exams take place simultaneously at all centres worldwide, time zones permitting.

Two notes on reading that table. The February rows print "A2 CELI i" where every other immigrati row prints "A2 CELI 1 i" — almost certainly a slip on the source page, but we flag rather than correct it. And the deadline column is not the whole story:

> "La scadenza per la presentazione delle domande di iscrizione è regolata dal Centro, che dovrà in ogni caso rispettare la scadenza indicata dal CVCL nel sito www.cvcl.it."
>
> *The deadline for submitting applications is set by the Centre, which must in any case respect the deadline indicated by CVCL.* — Regolamento degli esami CELI, art. 1.5

In practice that means your centre's deadline can be **earlier** than the published one, never later. Ask the centre, not the calendar.

## Planning backwards from a filing date

The certificate has to exist before the application does — for citizenship, the evidence is produced at the moment of filing. So work backwards through three intervals, not one.

- **Results.** Unistrasi's candidate-facing page says results are available after about three months, with no early assessments. Its manual for exam venues describes a window from 45 days — for B1 Cittadinanza only — to four months. Those two official statements disagree; plan against the longer one. CVCL states approximately 90 working days from the date it receives the scripts, which is not the date you sat.
- **The certificate itself.** CILS certificates are collected from the centre where you sat. CELI certificates are issued electronically by CVCL, sent to the centre and distributed by it; candidates needing proof sooner can download a provisional document attesting that they passed from their reserved area.
- **Your own margin.** Failing one CILS B1 Cittadinanza skill means re-sitting the entire exam at a later session. Book early enough that one failure does not break the plan.

One more deadline that is easy to miss: candidates requesting disability provisions for a CILS exam must send documentation during the enrolment period, or at the latest in the days immediately after it closes.

## Frequently asked

**If I miss a deadline, can the centre let me in anyway?**
Nothing official supports that. For CELI the centre is bound by the CVCL cut-off; for CILS the enrolment window is published with each session.

**What if I am ill on the day of a CELI exam?**
The Regolamento provides that a candidate prevented by illness presents a medical certificate, may sit at the immediately following session at the same centre, and is exempt from paying the fee again — but must still submit a proper enrolment application by the deadline. Other reasons for absence are assessed by CVCL.

**Are these dates the same everywhere?**
For CELI, the Regolamento says exams run simultaneously at all centres, time zones permitting. CILS sessions are set centrally by Siena for its affiliated venues.
