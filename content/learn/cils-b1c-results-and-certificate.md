How long is the wait? Siena answers that question in two places, with two different figures, and a citizenship candidate planning a filing date needs to see both.

The page written for candidates says: "I risultati degli esami saranno disponibili dopo circa 3 mesi. Non si eseguono valutazioni anticipate." (Exam results will be available after about 3 months. No early assessments are carried out.)

The manual Siena issues to exam centres describes a range instead, and singles out this module at the fast end of it: results are published "in un arco di tempo che può variare da 45 giorni (solo per il livello B1 modulo Cittadinanza) ai 4 mesi, a seconda dei livelli e del numero dei candidati della sessione" (over a period that can vary from 45 days — only for the B1 Cittadinanza module — to 4 months, depending on the levels and the number of candidates in the session).

Both are Siena's own words. We are not going to resolve them, and we are certainly not going to print "45 days" as a promise: it is the fast end of a range, in a document written for centres, and it is expressly conditioned on the level and the number of candidates in that session. Treat about three months as your planning assumption and anything faster as a welcome surprise.

## Checking your result

Results are published by the Centro CILS on the university's site. Siena's results page provides a lookup — "IL MIO RISULTATO" — which asks for your **matricola** (the candidate registration number issued when you enrol) and your **date of birth**. Keep the matricola somewhere you will still be able to find it three months later; it is the key to your own result.

## Collecting the certificate

The certificate is not posted to you by the university. Siena's instruction is that to collect it you must go to the centre where you sat the exam. We could not verify what happens if you have moved in the meantime, or whether any centre will forward it — ask your centre directly, and ideally before you move.

## Viewing your papers is not an appeal

Two different things get confused here, so it is worth separating them.

**What exists:** a right of access. Candidates may ask to see their exam papers within **30 days** of the publication of results, by writing to **trasparenzacertificazioni@unistrasi.it** with an identity document, personal details and the matricola, and paying the access fee provided for under **Law 241/90**.

**What we could not find:** any published re-mark, remarking or appeal procedure. We are phrasing that precisely — no official re-mark procedure is published on the pages we could reach. That is not the same as saying none exists, and it is not advice about your options.

Marking, in any case, happens centrally at the Centro CILS rather than at your exam centre, so a query about a mark is a query for Siena.

## Does the certificate expire?

Siena's FAQ answers this with a qualifier that deserves to survive translation: asked whether the certificate has an expiry date, it answers that **at present** it does not — the Italian word is *attualmente*, "currently". The university itself is not committing to permanence, and we will not commit on its behalf.

There is a separate question that this does not answer: whether a language certificate has any validity period **for citizenship purposes**, in the eyes of the authority receiving your application. No official source we could consult addresses that, in either direction. If your certificate is some years old, that is a question for the Prefettura or Consolato handling your application, not for us.

What is verified is the timing rule at the other end: art. 9.1 requires applicants to attest to the language requirement "all'atto della presentazione dell'istanza" — at the moment of filing the application. The certificate has to exist before you file, which is what makes the results wait part of your immigration timetable rather than an afterthought.

## Questions readers ask

**When exactly will my result appear?**
No date is published per session. About three months is the figure Siena gives candidates.

**Can I ask for an early result because of a filing deadline?**
The candidate-facing page states that early assessments are not carried out.

**Can someone else collect my certificate?**
Not addressed on the pages we could read. Ask your exam centre what it requires.

**Is a scan or photocopy enough for the application?**
That depends on the receiving authority, and requirements differ between the Prefettura and individual consulates. Check the current official page for the office handling your file.
